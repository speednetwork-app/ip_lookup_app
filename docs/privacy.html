隐私政策

皮卡云 VPN · 最后更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日

简要说明：IP 查询无需登录。你可以通过邮箱注册或登录，查看账号概览和订阅地址，并选择开启 VPN。 注册不会自动购买套餐；iOS 商城可在开放时通过 Apple 订阅。不含广告、第三方统计或用户追踪。

一、我们收集哪些信息

IP 查询功能不要求账号。如果你主动使用「我的」页面注册或登录，我们会处理你输入的 邮箱、密码及必要的验证信息，以创建账号、防止滥用或完成身份验证；后端要求邮箱验证时，会向你提供的邮箱发送验证码。登录后会获取该账号的套餐状态、每日流量趋势、有效期、使用限制和订阅地址。密码只通过 HTTPS 发送至账号服务器， 不会保存在设备上。本 App 不要求姓名、电话号码，也不会读取通讯录、相册、位置权限或 设备标识符。

二、数据如何被使用

为了完成查询，App 需要把待查询的 IP 地址发送给第三方 IP 数据库服务，并接收返回的 归属地信息。涉及的数据流向如下：

数据去向用途
你查询的 IP 地址 ipwho.is / ipapi.co 换取该 IP 的国家、地区、城市、时区与运营商信息
查询「我的 IP」时，你设备的公网 IP ipwho.is / ipapi.co 这是网络请求固有的——任何网站都能看到访问者的公网 IP

Apple 签名交易凭证、交易标识、商品、币种和标价、有效期、续订／退款状态，以及随机生成的账号绑定标识 Apple 与 api.pikayun.com 验证购买、绑定皮卡云账号、开通与恢复订阅、同步续订和撤销；不获取 Apple ID 密码或银行卡资料

IP 查询请求不会附带你的账号登录凭证；账号请求也不会附带本地查询历史。

三、数据存储在哪里

查询历史仅保存在你的设备本地，最多保留 50 条，不会同步到云端， 也不会被任何人（包括我们）读取。你可以在「历史」页左滑删除单条记录， 或点击右上角清空全部。历史入口位于「IP 检测」页右上角。卸载 App 会删除本机查询历史，不会取消 Apple 订阅或删除服务端交易记录。

登录成功后，登录凭证和账号邮箱保存在 iOS 钥匙串中，以便保持登录状态。密码不保存在 本机。套餐状态、流量趋势、有效期和使用限制会安全缓存于本机，以便网络异常时继续显示； 订阅地址不会写入该缓存，也不会写入查询历史。你可以在「我的」页退出登录， 删除本机保存的登录凭证、邮箱和账号缓存。用于恢复中断购买的最少操作记录（账号邮箱、随机操作编号、交易关联编号和待补报的操作结果）另行保存在钥匙串中，按账号隔离；不保存密码、签名交易凭证或付款资料，确认删除账户后会清除该账号的本机操作记录。复制订阅地址后，内容将进入系统剪贴板，并受系统剪贴板 机制管理。

服务端保存必要的订阅绑定、验签结果和交易记录，用于开通、恢复、对账及处理退款争议；收到的 Apple 签名通知加密保存。购买尝试、取消、待确认及验证结果会记录，用于恢复中断购买和防止重复下单。交易资料不用于广告追踪。退出登录或卸载不会删除这些服务端资料，也不会自动停止续费；你可以在 Apple 的订阅设置中管理或取消续订。

账户删除

登录后在「我的 → 删除账户」发起删除，核对说明、重新输入密码并确认。删除不是仅退出登录或暂时停用：将删除登录账户、邮箱和个人资料、非必要用量明细、工单内容，撤销全部设备的登录凭证与原订阅链接，终止该账号的剩余套餐使用权限。必要财务订单、交易资料及防重复认领标识按适用要求保留，以处理对账、退款和争议，不用于重新开通已删除账号或广告跟踪。数据清理遇到暂时故障时会重试，并在删除结果中说明。

删除账户不自动取消 Apple 订阅，也不自动退款。删除前请使用页面提供的 Apple 订阅管理入口取消不再需要的续订。本机 IP 查询历史不关联账号，可在隐私页面单独删除。尚有余额、佣金或正在处理的付款时，可在同一页面提交删除申请并查看进度；不会自动清零，结算完成后自动执行删除，无需重新申请。

Privacy Policy

Pikayun VPN · Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: IP lookup does not require an account. You may optionally register by email or sign in to view your account and subscription URL, and choose to use VPN. Registration does not purchase a plan. The iOS store offers Apple subscriptions when enabled. The app contains no ads, third-party analytics or user tracking.

1. Information We Collect

IP lookup does not require an account. If you choose to register or sign in on the Account screen, we process your email address, password and necessary verification information to create an account, prevent abuse or authenticate an existing account. When the server requires email verification, it sends a code to your email address. After authentication, the app retrieves account information and the subscription URL. The password is sent over HTTPS and is not stored on the device. The app does not request your name or phone number and never accesses contacts, photos, location permission or device identifiers.

2. How Data Is Used

To perform a lookup, the app sends the IP address being queried to a third-party IP database and receives the corresponding location details:

DataSent toPurpose
The IP address you look up ipwho.is / ipapi.co Resolve its country, region, city, time zone and network operator
Your device's public IP, when using "My IP" ipwho.is / ipapi.co Inherent to any network request — every website sees a visitor's public IP
Map viewport coordinates OpenStreetMap Load the map tiles for that area
Email, password, verification information and optional invitation code (only when you choose to register or sign in) api.pikayun.com Create or authenticate an account, prevent abuse and send verification codes; the password is not stored locally
Authentication credential and subscription URL api.pikayun.com Keep you signed in and display your subscription URL
Signed Apple transactions, transaction identifiers, products, currency and price, expiry, renewal/refund status and a random account-binding identifier Apple and api.pikayun.com Verify, link, activate and restore subscriptions, and reconcile renewals or revocations. We do not receive your Apple ID password or payment card details.

IP lookup requests do not include your account credential, and account requests do not include your local lookup history.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Lookup history is stored only on your device, capped at 50 entries. It is never synced to any cloud service and cannot be read by anyone, including us. You can swipe to delete individual entries or clear the entire history at any time, via the top-right History entry on IP Check. Deleting the app removes local lookup history, not Apple subscriptions or server-side transaction records.

After a successful sign-in, the authentication credential and account email are stored in the iOS Keychain to maintain the session. Your password is not stored. The subscription URL is fetched after sign-in and is not written to lookup history. Signing out from the Account screen deletes the locally stored credential and email. If you copy the subscription URL, it is placed on the system clipboard and handled by the operating system's clipboard mechanisms.

Minimal interrupted-purchase records (account email, random operation references, transaction correlation references and pending operation results) are separately stored in the Keychain, isolated per account. They do not contain passwords, signed receipts or payment details. Signing out retains these records for recovery; confirmed account deletion clears that account's local records.

We retain necessary server-side subscription bindings, verification results, purchase attempts and transaction records for activation, restoration, reconciliation, duplicate prevention and refund disputes. Received signed Apple notifications are encrypted at rest. These records are not used for advertising tracking. Signing out or deleting the app does not remove server-side records or cancel renewal. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple's subscription settings.

Account deletion

After signing in, open Account → Delete Account, review the consequences, re-enter your password and confirm. This deletes the login account, email, personal profile, nonessential usage details and support content, revokes all device sessions and the old subscription link, and ends remaining service access. Necessary financial orders, transaction records and anti-replay identifiers are retained under applicable financial and dispute requirements, not to recreate the account or track you for advertising. Temporary cleanup failures are retried and reflected in the result.

Account deletion does not cancel Apple subscriptions or automatically issue a refund. Use the subscription-management link before deleting. Local IP lookup history is independent and can be cleared separately. Accounts with a balance, commission or an in-flight payment can submit a deletion request and check its progress in the app. Funds are not automatically forfeited; deletion completes automatically after settlement, without a second request.
