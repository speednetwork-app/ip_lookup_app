隐私政策

IP 位置查询 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 16 日

简要说明：IP 查询无需登录。你可以选择登录已有账号来查看只读账号概览和订阅地址。 本 App 不提供注册或购买，不含广告、第三方统计或用户追踪。

一、我们收集哪些信息

IP 查询功能不要求账号。如果你主动使用「我的」页面登录已有账号，我们会处理你输入的 邮箱和密码以完成身份验证，并获取该账号的套餐状态、每日流量趋势、有效期、使用限制和订阅地址。密码只通过 HTTPS 发送至账号服务器， 不会保存在设备上。本 App 不要求姓名、电话号码，也不会读取通讯录、相册、位置权限或 设备标识符。

二、数据如何被使用

为了完成查询，App 需要把待查询的 IP 地址发送给第三方 IP 数据库服务，并接收返回的 归属地信息。涉及的数据流向如下：

数据去向用途
你查询的 IP 地址 ipwho.is / ipapi.co 换取该 IP 的国家、地区、城市、时区与运营商信息
查询「我的 IP」时，你设备的公网 IP ipwho.is / ipapi.co 这是网络请求固有的——任何网站都能看到访问者的公网 IP
地图区域坐标 OpenStreetMap 加载对应位置的地图图块
邮箱和密码（仅在主动登录时） api.pikayun.com 验证已有账号；密码不会保存在本机
登录凭证、账号概览、流量趋势和订阅地址 api.pikayun.com 保持登录状态，并向已登录用户显示其只读账号信息、系统公告和订阅地址

IP 查询请求不会附带你的账号登录凭证；账号请求也不会附带本地查询历史。

三、数据存储在哪里

查询历史仅保存在你的设备本地，最多保留 50 条，不会同步到云端， 也不会被任何人（包括我们）读取。你可以在「历史」页左滑删除单条记录， 或点击右上角清空全部。卸载 App 即彻底删除所有数据。

登录成功后，登录凭证和账号邮箱保存在 iOS 钥匙串中，以便保持登录状态。密码不保存在 本机。套餐状态、流量趋势、有效期和使用限制会安全缓存于本机，以便网络异常时继续显示； 订阅地址不会写入该缓存，也不会写入查询历史。你可以在「我的」页退出登录， 删除本机保存的登录凭证、邮箱和账号缓存。复制订阅地址后，内容将进入系统剪贴板，并受系统剪贴板 机制管理。

四、网络服务

本 App 连接以下网络服务：

  • api.pikayun.com —— 已有账号登录、只读账号概览、流量趋势、系统公告和订阅地址
  • ipwho.is —— IP 归属地数据
  • ipapi.co —— IP 归属地数据（备用源）
  • OpenStreetMap —— 地图图块

五、我们不做的事

  • 不含任何广告或广告 SDK
  • 不含任何分析、埋点或崩溃统计工具
  • 不进行用户追踪，不请求 App 跟踪透明度（ATT）授权
  • 不出售、使用或向第三方披露账号数据、登录凭证或订阅地址；这些数据仅用于向你提供登录和只读账号信息展示功能
  • 不提供账号注册、套餐购买或应用内购买
  • 不申请定位、相机、麦克风、通讯录等任何系统权限

六、儿童隐私

本 App 不针对 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的任何信息。

Privacy Policy

IP Lookup · Last updated: 13 October 2026

In short: IP lookup does not require an account. You may optionally sign in to an existing account to view its subscription URL. The app offers no registration or purchasing and contains no ads, third-party analytics or user tracking.

1. Information We Collect

IP lookup does not require an account. If you choose to sign in on the Account screen, we process the email address and password you enter to authenticate your existing account and retrieve its subscription URL. The password is sent over HTTPS and is not stored on the device. The app does not request your name or phone number and never accesses contacts, photos, location permission or device identifiers.

2. How Data Is Used

To perform a lookup, the app sends the IP address being queried to a third-party IP database and receives the corresponding location details:

DataSent toPurpose
The IP address you look up ipwho.is / ipapi.co Resolve its country, region, city, time zone and network operator
Your device's public IP, when using "My IP" ipwho.is / ipapi.co Inherent to any network request — every website sees a visitor's public IP
Map viewport coordinates OpenStreetMap Load the map tiles for that area
Email and password (only when you choose to sign in) api.pikayun.com Authenticate an existing account; the password is not stored locally
Authentication credential and subscription URL api.pikayun.com Keep you signed in and display your subscription URL

IP lookup requests do not include your account credential, and account requests do not include your local lookup history.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Lookup history is stored only on your device, capped at 50 entries. It is never synced to any cloud service and cannot be read by anyone, including us. You can swipe to delete individual entries or clear the entire history at any time. Deleting the app removes all of it permanently.

After a successful sign-in, the authentication credential and account email are stored in the iOS Keychain to maintain the session. Your password is not stored. The subscription URL is fetched after sign-in and is not written to lookup history. Signing out from the Account screen deletes the locally stored credential and email. If you copy the subscription URL, it is placed on the system clipboard and handled by the operating system's clipboard mechanisms.

4. Network Services

  • api.pikayun.com — existing-account authentication and subscription URL
  • ipwho.is — IP geolocation data
  • ipapi.co — IP geolocation data (fallback)
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles

5. What We Do Not Do

  • No advertising or ad SDKs
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash-reporting tools
  • No user tracking; we never request App Tracking Transparency permission
  • No selling, use or disclosure of account data, authentication credentials or subscription URLs to third parties; this data is used only to provide sign-in and subscription URL display to you
  • No account registration, plan purchasing or in-app purchases
  • No location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions requested

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
